Real hardware, early

Prototypes on the bench, not roadmaps. You will see the thing you designed being built, tested and taken apart again — often in the same month.

Work that matters clinically

We develop for a procedure that today demands extraordinary manual skill. Our goal is to make that capability easier to deliver — a long road, and we are at the beginning of it.

Scientific environment

Spin-off of TU Dresden, University Hospital Carl Gustav Carus Dresden and the Leibniz Institute of Polymer Research Dresden — with the labs, equipment and expertise that come with those ties.

Hiring process

What to expect

Step 1

Your application

CV and a short note on what draws you to the role. A cover letter is welcome but not required — we would rather read three honest sentences than three polished paragraphs.

Step 2

First conversation

A video call with one or two of us: your background, what we are building, and whether the role is what you think it is.

Step 3

On site in Dresden

A deeper technical discussion, a look at the lab, and time with the people you would work alongside. Then a decision — quickly, either way.

Nothing matching your profile?

We are a young company and roles are still taking shape. If you build things that have to work in the physical world, we would like to hear from you anyway.
